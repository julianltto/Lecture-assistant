# Some future ideas

- Show a few example questions on the start page, so that new users know what to ask.
- Get more students to use the site
- 39 answers per week is too few to train a model, even for the synthesization
- Possibly, stop using the lecture notes as the basis for the answers and let the model answer freely. Then the answers would no longer depend on one script, and I could collect training data from other sources, e.g. Math StackExchange.
